# Further resources: Unit 11

- **Martin Fowler: "Monolith First" and "MicroservicePremium"** (articles, martinfowler.com) — short, opinionated pieces on when microservices pay off; the premium article quantifies what you pay.
- **Sam Newman, "Building Microservices" (2nd ed.), Ch. 3 (Splitting the Monolith) and Ch. 4 (Microservice Communication Styles)** (book) — the most practical guide to strangler fig, data migration, and communication patterns.
- **Vaughn Vernon, "Domain-Driven Design Distilled"** (book) — a short introduction to bounded contexts and aggregates, enough to draw boundaries without reading the full DDD tome.
- **Pact documentation: "Consumer-driven contracts"** (docs, docs.pact.io) — a hands-on way to test service contracts in CI; pairs with the expand/migrate/contract discipline.
- **Team Topologies (Skelton & Pais), Part I** (book) — connects team structure and architecture (Conway's Law), useful for arguing about service boundaries with organizational reality.
