# Further resources: Unit 14

- **Michael Nygard, "Documenting Architecture Decisions"** (blog post, cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions) — the original ADR proposal; short and exactly the format used here.
- **Google SRE Book, Ch. 15: "Postmortem Culture: Learning from Failure"** (book chapter, free at sre.google) — the definitive description of blameless postmortems and how to make them stick.
- **"Accelerate" (Forsgren, Humble, Kim)** (book) — the research behind DORA metrics; use it to justify investments in deploy frequency, lead time, change failure rate, and MTTR.
- **Will Larson, "Staff Engineer" and the "StaffEng" guides (staffeng.com)** (book/site) — practical guidance on writing design docs, leading reviews, and exercising technical judgment as a senior/staff engineer.
- **Joel Spolsky, "Things You Should Never Do, Part I"** (essay, joelonsoftware.com) — the classic argument against big-bang rewrites; pairs with the strangler-fig material from Unit 11.
