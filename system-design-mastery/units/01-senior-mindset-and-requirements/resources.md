# Further resources: Unit 1

- **Google SRE Book, Ch. 4: "Service Level Objectives"** (book chapter, free at sre.google/sre-book/service-level-objectives/) — the canonical treatment of SLIs/SLOs/SLAs; read it after this lecture to see real-world SLI choices and why percentiles matter.
- **Google SRE Workbook, Ch. 2: "Implementing SLOs"** (book chapter, free at sre.google/workbook/implementing-slos/) — the practical follow-up: how to pick SLIs per user journey and set targets, which is exactly the skill you'll need at work.
- **Designing Data-Intensive Applications, Ch. 1: "Reliable, Scalable, and Maintainable Applications"** (Martin Kleppmann, book) — the best short framing of NFRs (reliability, scalability, maintainability) and includes the tail-latency discussion. Reread it after Unit 7.
- **"The Tail at Scale"** (Dean & Barroso, paper, CACM 2013) — the source for tail-latency amplification; short and readable, and it previews the hedging techniques you'll use in Unit 10.
- **Martin Fowler: "Monolith First"** (article, martinfowler.com/bliki/MonolithFirst.html) — a concise argument for "start simple, add complexity against named requirements," which we'll return to in Unit 11.
